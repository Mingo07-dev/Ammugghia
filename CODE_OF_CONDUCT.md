# Code of conduct

Ammugghia is a small project maintained in spare time. Everyone who takes part — in issues,
pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented — is expected to make
it a welcoming and useful place.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent; people write in English or Italian, often not in
  their first language.
- Keep feedback about the work, specific and constructive.
- Accept that a proposal may be declined, especially when it trades safety for convenience:
  Ammugghia deletes files, and "when in doubt, refuse" applies to the project too.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized language or content, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated off-topic posts after being asked to stop.
- Posting commands or advice meant to make someone lose data.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, [@Mingo07-dev](https://github.com/Mingo07-dev),
through the [private reporting form](https://github.com/Mingo07-dev/Ammugghia/security/advisories/new)
(mention that it is a conduct report), or use GitHub's *Report content* option on the comment
or issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code,
and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a participant, depending on how serious
and how repeated the behavior is.

This code applies to all project spaces, and to public spaces when someone is representing
the project. It is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
